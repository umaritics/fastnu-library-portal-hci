How	to	read	this	document
Evidence	IDs.	P03-C2	means	participant	P03,	Segment	C	(observed	booking	attempt),	note	2.	Segments	follow	D1A:	A	background,	B	retrospective	booking,	C	observed	booking	attempt,	D	book	search,	E	workarounds	and	closing.	Every	ID	is
listed	in	sections	1.1	and	1.1b.	SV	and	S	IDs	are	survey	cards	(section	1.1b).
Other	IDs.	K	=	affinity	cluster,	T	=	theme,	I	=	insight,	N	=	need,	U	=	user	story,	R	=	requirement,	HMW	=	How-Might-We,	POV	=	point-of-view	statement.	Numbers	such	as	2.3	inside	the	task	analysis	are	task-analysis	nodes.
Quotations	are	exactly	as	typed	in	D1B	or	in	the	survey.	[uncertain]	is	carried	over	from	D1B	where	the	note-taker	and	facilitator	could	not	agree	on	what	was	said	or	done.
Participants	appear	only	as	P01	to	P10,	and	survey	respondents	only	as	S01	to	S09.	No	names,	roll	numbers	or	identifying	details	appear.
Nothing	in	this	document	describes	a	solution.	Needs,	opportunities	and	requirements	are	outcomes	only.
Core	task	and	scope.
	The	core	task	is	booking	a	library	study	room.	D1A	made	it	the	primary	research	question	(RQ1),	and	nine	of	the	ten	interview	participants	used	or	described	a	route	that	skips	the	portal	for	room	booking	(P07	is	the
exception).	Book	search	(RQ2)	is	carried	through	the	synthesis	as	theme	T4	and	kept	as	a	secondary	finding	for	later	stages.	It	is	not	carried	into	the	POV	or	the	requirements.
